2. Data that stays on your phone

The app works without an account and offline. Unless you switch on a feature from section 3, the following data is stored only in the database on your phone and we have no access to it:

  • Profile: name or nickname, year of birth, height, sex (optional, only used for rest length between sets), units, goals, settings.
  • Workouts: plans, sets, weights, reps, exercise notes, records, badges, how you felt after a workout.
  • Body weight and water intake.
  • Periods: illness, injury (including the muscle group), cutting, bulking. This is health data, so it is never shared with friends or in leaderboards.
  • Calendar (only if you allow it): the app reads event times to find free time for a workout and adds planned workouts to your calendar. Events never leave your phone.
  • Health Connect / Apple Health (only if you allow it): the app writes workouts and calories burned and reads and writes body weight. We do not use data from Health Connect or Apple Health for advertising and we do not sell it. Imported body weight becomes part of the app data, so it reaches our servers only in the automatic backup or when you share body weight with your coach, if you turn them on.
  • Camera (only if you allow it): used only to scan a friend’s or gym’s QR code on the phone; images are not stored.
  • Location (only if you allow it): used only to find gyms near you. Your exact location stays on the phone: the app turns it into grid areas (roughly 1–5 km) and uses them to load gyms from the server. We don’t store your location or track it in the background. Exception: if you choose “I’m at this gym right now” when adding a gym, the gym’s location is saved (see part 3).
  • Wear OS watch: the phone sends the current exercise and set to your paired watch and the watch sends back completed sets. The transfer runs through Google’s service between your own devices, not through our servers.
  • Manual backup and CSV export: you create the file and decide where to save it or who to send it to.

You can delete this data by uninstalling the app or in Profile → Delete all data.

Body weight, illness or injury periods and water amounts are never shared with friends. In a water challenge your friend only sees the percentage completed. Only members of a gym see its leaderboard and only your friends see your profile; there is no user search. Anyone who picks a gym in the app can join its leaderboard, so use a nickname you are happy for other gym-goers to see.

Any member can print the gym leaderboard for the gym’s notice board (top 5 in four exercises, men and women). The printout shows only nicknames and lifts of members who left “Show me on the printed leaderboard” on; it never shows names, photos, age or anything else.

How I rank: once a day we compute anonymous distributions of the heaviest lifts from visible gym leaderboard entries, per country of the gym and for the whole world, split by gender and age group. They contain no nicknames or accounts, and a group with fewer than 20 people shows only its size. We publish only rounded percentiles (5th to 95th, to 2.5 kg), never individual values. The app compares your own best lifts with them on your phone; nothing extra is sent for that.

Coach: only a coach you connect with yourself and agree to share with sees your workouts (in the app and on the coach site gymbar.app/trener). The coach does not see notes, how a workout felt, illness or injury periods, water intake or other Apple Health / Health Connect data; body weight only with your separate consent. A coach is an independent person, not our employee, and is responsible for what they do outside the app with what they see. When the connection ends, your workouts, weight, plans and comments are deleted for the coach automatically; plans saved on your phone stay there. Anyone with the link can open a plan shared by link.

Only the app developer sees reports; the reported person is not told. A gym reported by five people whose accounts are at least 7 days old is hidden from the gym list; a gym without members also after five reports that it doesn’t exist or is a duplicate (it shows up again once someone joins it).

The gym list comes from the public OpenStreetMap database (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL licence) and is completed by users. It contains no personal data.

An automatic check hides unrealistic lifts in the gym leaderboard, and an entry is hidden once members report it (a third of the members, at least 3 and at most 5; only members who have been in the gym for at least 7 days and have their own entry can report). Other members cannot read a hidden entry. This only affects visibility in the leaderboard, has no other effect, and your data on your phone stays unchanged.

2. Dati che restano sul telefono

L’app funziona senza account e offline. Se non attivi una funzione della sezione 3, i dati seguenti sono salvati solo nel database sul tuo telefono e noi non vi abbiamo accesso:

  • Profilo: nome o nickname, anno di nascita, altezza, sesso (facoltativo, usato solo per la durata del recupero tra le serie), unità, obiettivi, impostazioni.
  • Allenamenti: schede, serie, carichi, ripetizioni, note sugli esercizi, record, badge, la sensazione dopo l’allenamento.
  • Peso corporeo e idratazione.
  • Periodi: malattia, infortunio (compreso il gruppo muscolare), definizione, massa. Sono dati sulla salute, quindi non vengono mai condivisi con gli amici né nelle classifiche.
  • Calendario (solo se lo consenti): l’app legge gli orari degli eventi per trovare tempo libero per allenarti e aggiunge gli allenamenti pianificati al calendario. Gli eventi non lasciano mai il telefono.
  • Health Connect / Apple Salute (solo se lo consenti): l’app scrive allenamenti e calorie bruciate e legge e scrive il peso corporeo. Non usiamo i dati di Health Connect o Apple Salute per la pubblicità e non li vendiamo. Il peso importato diventa parte dei dati dell’app, quindi arriva sui nostri server solo nel backup automatico o quando condividi il peso con il tuo coach, se li attivi.
  • Fotocamera (solo se lo consenti): usata solo per scansionare il codice QR di un amico o di una palestra sul telefono; le immagini non vengono salvate.
  • Posizione (solo se lo consenti): usata solo per trovare palestre vicino a te. La posizione esatta resta sul telefono: l’app la trasforma in aree di una griglia (circa 1–5 km) e le usa per caricare le palestre dal server. Non salviamo la tua posizione e non la tracciamo in background. Eccezione: se scegli «Sono in questa palestra adesso» quando aggiungi una palestra, viene salvata la posizione della palestra (vedi sezione 3).
  • Orologio Wear OS: il telefono invia all’orologio associato l’esercizio e la serie in corso e l’orologio rimanda le serie completate. Il trasferimento passa per il servizio di Google tra i tuoi dispositivi, non per i nostri server.
  • Backup manuale ed esportazione CSV: sei tu a creare il file e a decidere dove salvarlo o a chi inviarlo.

Puoi cancellare questi dati disinstallando l’app o in Profilo → Elimina tutti i dati.

Gli altri membri non possono leggere una voce nascosta. Questo riguarda solo la visibilità in classifica, non ha altri effetti e i tuoi dati sul telefono restano invariati.

Ze bevatten geen bijnamen of accounts, en van een groep met minder dan 20 mensen wordt alleen de grootte getoond. We publiceren alleen afgeronde percentielen (5e tot 95e, op 2,5 kg), nooit afzonderlijke waarden. De app vergelijkt je eigen beste prestaties daarmee op je telefoon; daarvoor wordt niets extra verstuurd.

Coach: alleen een coach met wie je zelf koppelt en met wie je akkoord gaat om te delen, ziet je trainingen (in de app en op de website voor coaches gymbar.app/trener). De coach ziet geen notities, hoe een training voelde, periodes van ziekte of blessure, waterinname of andere gegevens uit Apple Gezondheid / Health Connect; lichaamsgewicht alleen met je aparte toestemming. Een coach is een zelfstandig persoon, niet bij ons in dienst, en is zelf verantwoordelijk voor wat hij of zij buiten de app doet met wat hij of zij ziet. Als de koppeling eindigt, worden je trainingen, gewicht, schema’s en opmerkingen automatisch bij de coach verwijderd; schema’s die op je telefoon zijn opgeslagen, blijven daar. Iedereen met de link kan een via een link gedeeld schema openen.

Alleen de ontwikkelaar van de app ziet meldingen; de gemelde persoon krijgt dat niet te horen. Een gym die is gemeld door vijf mensen van wie het account minstens 7 dagen oud is, wordt verborgen in de lijst met gyms; een gym zonder leden ook na vijf meldingen dat hij niet bestaat of dubbel is (hij verschijnt weer zodra iemand zich aanmeldt).

De lijst met gyms komt uit de openbare database OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap-bijdragers, ODbL-licentie) en wordt aangevuld door gebruikers. Hij bevat geen persoonsgegevens.

Een automatische controle verbergt onrealistische prestaties in de gym-ranglijst, en een item wordt verborgen zodra leden het melden (een derde van de leden, minimaal 3 en maximaal 5; alleen leden die minstens 7 dagen lid zijn van de gym en zelf een item hebben, kunnen melden). Andere leden kunnen een verborgen item niet lezen. Dit heeft alleen invloed op de zichtbaarheid in de ranglijst, heeft geen ander gevolg en je gegevens op je telefoon blijven ongewijzigd.

Elas não contêm apelidos nem contas, e um grupo com menos de 20 pessoas mostra só o tamanho. Publicamos só percentis arredondados (do 5.º ao 95.º, de 2,5 em 2,5 kg), nunca valores individuais.
