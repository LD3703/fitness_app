A gym log that keeps track of your progress for you.

Log a set with two taps, let the app suggest next time's weight and watch yourself get stronger. It works without an account and offline, and your data stays on your phone.

TRAINING
• Your own plans and ready-made programs; pick your training days right away
• Sets, warm-ups, supersets and drop sets
• Rest timer
• Exercise library with instructions and demos
• Plan B: a short home routine when you can't make it to the gym

PROGRESS
• Auto progression: after a workout, the weight or reps to aim for next time
• Progressive overload check for each muscle group, with badges for consistency
• Personal records (your heaviest set), volume and muscle group frequency
• Muscle fatigue: which muscle groups still need rest
• Ill, injured or cutting? Log the period and the charts take it into account

YOUR GYM AND FRIENDS
• Your gym's leaderboard: join with the QR code at reception or from the list
• Men's and women's categories and age groups 40+, 50+ and 60+
• "Beat my record" challenges, workout invitations and streaks with friends

MORE
• Coach tone: friendly, or strict to the point of sarcasm
• Water intake, body weight and reminders
• Calendar: finds free time for a workout and adds it
• Apple Health
• Dark mode, kilograms and pounds, 10 languages

PRIVACY
Workouts, body weight and illness or injury records stay on your phone. Only what you switch on goes to our servers: friends, the gym leaderboard or cloud backup. No ads.

GymBar is not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before you start training, especially if you have health problems.
